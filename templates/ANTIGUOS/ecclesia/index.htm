WordPress theme for church websites

Ecclesia is a WordPress theme created with church websites in mind. Flexible and easy to use, this theme will help you bring your church website to the next level.

  • All theme colors and backgrounds can be easily changed in the options panel (using color pickers)
  • 17 custom widgets, each witch individual color and layout opions
